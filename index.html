AI-Powered Dynamic Mental Health Monitoring and Distress Prediction System for Victims of Atrocities

A compassionate computational framework designed to identify early psychological distress warning signs and facilitate intervention pathways.

Problem Statement

Victims of atrocities frequently experience profound psychological trauma, delayed distress manifestation, and limited access to immediate mental health support. Conventional monitoring lacks timely prediction capabilities.

Project Vision & Proposed Solution

Conceptualized a dynamic, privacy-conscious monitoring framework utilizing adaptive indicators and pattern recognition to detect emotional distress escalation early, connecting individuals to supportive resources.

My Leadership & Coordination Responsibilities

  • Collaboration Management: Facilitated open technical discussions, synchronized progress, and resolved blockers under pressure.

Outcomes & Learnings

📐 Collaborative Project Role: Integration & Quality

Plan2Space — Transforming 2D Floor Plans into Interactive 3D Layouts

Bridging the gap between static architectural drawings and spatial comprehension through interactive 3D scene generation.

The Problem

Traditional 2D blueprints are difficult for non-architects to interpret accurately. Understanding depth, daylighting, and spatial volume requires spatial visualization tools that turn flat floor plans into navigable spaces.

The 3D Visualization Concept

Plan2Space takes 2D layout inputs, parses structural boundaries, and translates them into an interactive 3D spatial model for intuitive exploration and walkthroughs.

My Integration Responsibilities

  • Project & Repository Structure: Structured the collaborative Git repository and established clean component hierarchy.
  • Coordinating Shared Interfaces: Defined boundary data contracts connecting 2D parsing modules to the 3D scene renderer.
  • AI Output to 3D Viewer Planning: Planned and verified the data pipeline converting parsed geometries into scene meshes.
  • Quality Assurance & Testing: Tested edge cases, verified coordinate transformations, and ensured seamless component communication.

Outcomes & Learnings

Hands-on mastery of interface contracts, multi-developer Git hygiene, integration testing, and translating algorithmic outputs into interactive 3D visuals.
